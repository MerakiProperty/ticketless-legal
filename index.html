How it works

Built for the car parks that catch people out

Barrierless, pay-online-later car parks are where charges slip through — this is the exact gap TicketLess closes.

📍

Detects your arrival automatically

UK train stations and major airport drop-off zones are already built in. Add anywhere else — a supermarket, gym, pub, hotel — in seconds.

🔔

Reminds you before it's too late

A reminder lands on your lock screen — not buried in-app — with one tap through to pay, mark it paid, or say you're not parking after all.

📄

Helps if one slips through anyway

Your parking history is kept for weeks. If a Parking Charge Notice arrives late, TicketLess helps draft a POPLA/IAS-format appeal letter in minutes.

🔒

No account, ever

There's nothing to sign up for. Your car parks, vehicles, and history live only on your device — not on a server, not for sale.

✈️

Covers airport drop-offs too

Heathrow, Gatwick, Manchester and more charge for dropping someone off, paid online the next day — easy to forget, easy to get fined for.

✍️

Register-to-park sites, remembered

Some car parks are free but only if you register your reg. Tell TicketLess once, and it reminds you the same way every visit after — so a free stay doesn't quietly turn into a charge.

Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. TicketLess has no login of any kind. Your data lives only on your device.

Does TicketLess pay on my behalf?

No — it never logs into or pays a parking operator for you. It opens the operator's own app or site so you pay in your own account, then tracks that you've done it.

Which car parks are covered automatically?

UK train stations and major UK airport drop-off zones are built in. Anywhere else, add it once and TicketLess watches for you from then on.

Is it available on Android?

Not yet — TicketLess is iOS-only at launch. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for the complete details on how the app works.
